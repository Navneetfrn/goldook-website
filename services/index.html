Services

What you can do with Goldook

Most of Goldook is free. When a service costs money, you see the full price before you pay.

Gold portfolio Free

Every piece in one private list: photos, bills, weight, purity and hallmark (HUID). Scan a bill and the details fill themselves in.

  • Photos of each piece and its bill, kept with the item
  • Weight, purity (carat), making charges, where and when you bought it
  • Check a BIS hallmark (HUID) number
  • Scan a bill to fill in the details for you; you check everything before saving

Live gold value Free

See what your gold and silver are worth at today's price, updated every hour, with price alerts when the rate crosses a level you choose.

  • 24K, 22K and 18K gold, silver and more, in rupees per gram
  • Profit or loss on each item since you bought it
  • Alerts when a price goes above or below the level you set

Gold loan calculator Free

Work out the EMI before you visit a lender, compare the published rates of banks and NBFCs side by side, and download an estimate to take with you.

  • Monthly EMI, interest-only and bullet repayment compared
  • Published rates of banks and NBFCs side by side
  • A PDF estimate with the questions to ask the lender

Nominees & Will Will PDF free · legal Will paid

Decide who each item goes to and make a Will draft as a PDF, with your gold listed item by item. Want it done properly? A verified lawyer finalises it and can register it.

  • Name nominees and decide who gets each item or what share
  • A Will draft PDF listing your gold, ready to sign before two witnesses
  • Optional legal Will: a verified advocate finalises it, arranges signing before two witnesses, and registers it if you want, with the fee shown before you pay

Gift gold & legal gift deed Gift deed PDF free · legal deed paid

Give an item to family through the app and make a gift deed PDF. Want it legally executed? A verified advocate prepares it on stamp paper for you.

  • The person receiving the gift accepts it in their own Goldook account
  • Free gift deed PDF; undo a gift until it's accepted
  • Optional legal gift deed on stamp paper by a verified advocate, with the fee shown before you pay

Polishing & repair Small booking fee

Book gold polishing or repair with verified jewellers near you. Pay a small booking fee, then agree the work and price with the jeweller directly.

  • Only jewellers we have checked are listed
  • After you pay the booking fee you get the jeweller's address and phone, and they are told you're coming
  • You agree the work and price with the jeweller and pay them at the shop; Goldook never handles your gold
