Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Goldook safe?

Yes. Every connection uses encrypted HTTPS, your records are stored encrypted on servers in Mumbai, India, and database rules let only you read your items, photos and bills. The website is protected by Cloudflare's firewall, the app can be locked with a PIN or fingerprint, and Goldook never asks for your PIN, OTP, password or card details. How we keep you safe.

Can Goldook's team see my gold?

No. Your list of items, photos and bills is visible only to you. Our team sees only a rough holding range (for example "₹1 to 5 lakh") to understand how the app is used, never your items.

How does Goldook calculate the value of my gold?

Weight in grams × today's gold rate for its purity. For example, 22 carat is 22/24 of the 24 carat rate. Rates are refreshed every hour. The value is the gold only: making charges and GST are not included. See today's gold rate.

Does Goldook hold my gold or give loans?

No. Goldook never takes, holds, lends against or sells your gold. The gold loan calculator shows estimates and published lender rates so you can compare before you visit a bank or NBFC yourself.

How do I make a Will for my gold in India?

List each item, choose who gets it, then sign the Will in front of two witnesses who also sign. Registration is optional. Goldook lists your gold item by item and makes the Will draft for you; a verified lawyer can finalise it. Read the full guide.

Is Goldook free?

Yes. The portfolio, live value, price alerts, loan calculator, nominees, Will PDF and gift deed PDF are free. Paid extras: a legal gift deed or legal Will prepared by a verified advocate, and a small booking fee for jeweller polishing or repair.

Which languages and devices does Goldook support?

English and 16 Indian languages, including Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Urdu. It works in any web browser at goldook.com/app and can be installed on a computer or phone from there. iPhone and Android store apps are coming soon.
