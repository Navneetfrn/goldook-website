Goldook

Your gold, in one place

Goldook keeps a record of the gold and silver you own, shows today's prices, and helps you estimate a gold loan before you visit a lender.

What you can do

  • List your jewellery and coins with photos, bills and hallmark numbers.
  • See what your gold is worth at today's price.
  • Work out a gold loan EMI and compare lenders.
  • Note who each item should go to.

Goldook does not lend money or take payments.

Open Goldook in your browser

Works on any computer or phone. Sign in with the same account you use in the app.
