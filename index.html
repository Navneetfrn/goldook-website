Goldook

Your gold, in one place

Goldook keeps a record of the gold and silver you own, shows today's prices, and helps you estimate a gold loan before you visit a lender.

What you can do

  • List your jewellery and coins with photos, bills and hallmark numbers.
  • See what your gold is worth at today's price.
  • Work out a gold loan EMI and compare lenders.
  • Note who each item should go to, and make a Will or gift deed PDF.
  • Give an item to family through the app, with a legal gift deed if you want one.
  • Book gold polishing and repair with verified jewellers near you.

Goldook does not lend money. You only pay for services you choose, such as a jeweller booking fee or a legal gift deed.
