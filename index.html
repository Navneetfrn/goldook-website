How it works

Set up in minutes, useful for years

  1. Add your goldTake a photo, scan the bill, or type the weight and purity. Add the hallmark number if it has one.
  2. See its value todayGoldook works out what each piece is worth at the current rate, and what you've gained since you bought it.
  3. Plan what's nextEstimate a loan, name nominees, make a Will draft, give an item to family, or book polishing and repair.

Privacy

Built so that only you know what you own

  • Your items, photos and bills are visible only to you. Our own team sees only a value range, never your list.
  • Lock the app with a PIN or your fingerprint, and hide amounts with one tap when someone is looking.
  • Nothing is shared with a bank, lender or jeweller unless you ask for it, and you see exactly what goes.
  • Delete your account and everything in it whenever you like.
