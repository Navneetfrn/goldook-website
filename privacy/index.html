Privacy Policy

Goldook · Last updated 22 November 2026

1. Who we are

Goldook is run by the Goldook team. Questions about this policy: support@goldook.com.

2. What we collect

Only what the app needs to work:

  • Your mobile number, if you sign in with a one-time code
  • Your name, email address and profile picture, if you sign in with Google or Apple (Apple may give us a private relay email instead)
  • Profile details you choose to add: name, email, date of birth, gender and photo
  • What you add to the app: gold and silver items, their photos and receipts, existing loans, saved loan estimates, and any nominees you list (their name, relation, mobile number and share, and which items go to whom). Nominees are not contacted and get no access to your account. Details you type to make a Will PDF (address, age, parent's or spouse's name) are used on your phone only and are not sent to us
  • Invoices you scan: the photo or PDF is read to fill in the item form and is not kept, unless you save the item with it as its receipt
  • A notification token for your phone, only if you turn on EMI reminders
  • Your answer to "How likely are you to recommend Goldook?" and any comment, only if you choose to answer
  • If you joined from someone's invite link, who invited you (so we can count invites)
  • Your PAN and the name printed on it, if you choose to verify your PAN. We send them to our verification partner, and keep only the last four characters of the PAN and a one-way fingerprint of it, never the full number

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your Aadhaar or bank account details, and we do not fetch your credit score
  • We do not track your location. The store finder opens Google Maps, which has its own privacy policy
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not sell your data. We only note the days you open the app (the date, nothing else) to count daily users
  • Price alerts and your wishlist stay on your phone and are not sent to us

4. Who handles your data for us

We use these service providers, only to run the app:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in the Mumbai (India) region
  • Our SMS provider: sends your one-time sign-in code
  • Google and Apple: only if you choose to sign in with them
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers EMI reminders to your phone
  • Anthropic (Claude): reads invoices you choose to scan, to fill in the item form. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models
  • Decentro: checks your PAN and the name on it with the Income Tax Department, only when you ask to verify your PAN

5. We do not share your details with lenders

Goldook compares the gold loan rates that lenders publish on their own websites. It does not send your details to any lender. If you choose a lender, you apply with that lender directly, and they handle your data under their own privacy policy.

6. How we protect your data

  • All connections between the app and our servers use HTTPS
  • Database rules allow each account to read and change only its own records
  • Only Goldook's named admins can see other people's accounts: your name, contact details and items. They use this only to give support, to contact you about your account and Goldook, and to fix problems
  • Photos and receipts are kept in private storage and opened with links that expire after one hour
  • You can turn on App lock (a PIN, fingerprint or Face ID) and hide amounts on screen. Your PIN stays on your phone; we never receive it
  • Our hosting provider encrypts stored data
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires

7. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account is open. Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account removes your account, your records and your files from our database straight away. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed when those backups expire, usually within a few weeks. Details already sent to a lender are held by that lender under its own policy.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and complain to us. Write to support@goldook.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Age

Goldook is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change affects data we already hold.
