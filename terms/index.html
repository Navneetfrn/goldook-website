2. Who can use it

3. What the app does

  • Tracks the gold and silver you own and what it is worth at current prices
  • Shows gold, silver, platinum and palladium prices and charts
  • Estimates gold loan amounts and EMIs, and keeps track of loans you already have
  • Reminds you before an EMI is due, if you turn reminders on
  • Compares the gold loan rates lenders publish, with a link to each lender's own website. You apply with the lender directly
  • Lets you give an item to someone through their Goldook account, make a gift deed PDF, and ask for a legal gift deed prepared by an independent, verified advocate. Goldook is not a law firm and does not give legal advice; the advocate is responsible for the legal work. You see the full fee before you pay; Goldook keeps a platform fee from it and pays the rest to the advocate
  • Lets you book gold polishing or repair with an independent jeweller that Goldook has checked. The booking fee is paid to Goldook and is not refundable once the jeweller's details are shared with you. You agree the work and its price with the jeweller directly and pay them yourself. Goldook never takes or holds your gold and is not responsible for the jeweller's work, the condition of your item, or any dispute about it
  • Lets you ask a verified advocate to finalise your Will, arrange signing before two witnesses, and register it if you choose. A Will needs no stamp paper and registration is optional. The advocate is responsible for the legal work; you see the full fee before you pay, and Goldook keeps a platform fee from it

4. Prices and estimates are indicative

Prices come from public market data and are converted to rupees per gram with an estimate for Indian import duty and GST. They are not the price a jeweller or bank will give you. Loan estimates are not offers. Nothing in the app is financial, tax or legal advice.
