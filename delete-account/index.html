Delete your account

Last updated 26 November 2026

You can delete your Goldook account and all of its data at any time.

In the app

  • Open Goldook and sign in.
  • Go to Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account.
  • Type DELETE to confirm.

Without the app

Email support@goldook.com from the email address on your account, or tell us the mobile number you sign in with. We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile, portfolio items, photos and receipts, existing loans, saved estimates, reminder settings, PAN verification record, loan application records, and sanction letters you added.
  • This happens straight away. Copies in our hosting provider's backups expire within a few weeks.
  • Details you already chose to send to a lender are held by that lender under its own policy.
