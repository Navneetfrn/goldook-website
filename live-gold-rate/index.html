Questions about gold rates

What is the gold rate today in India?

The table on this page shows today's indicative rate per gram and per 10 grams for 24, 22 and 18 carat gold and per kilogram for silver. It refreshes every hour from international prices converted to rupees, including the usual Indian market premium.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 18K gold?

Carat measures purity out of 24. 24K is 99.9% pure gold (coins and bars). 22K is 91.6% pure (marked 916) and is used for most Indian jewellery. 18K is 75% pure (marked 750) and is common in diamond and studded jewellery.

Why is my jeweller's rate different?

Jewellers add their own margin and city rates vary a little. When you buy jewellery you also pay making charges and 3% GST, which are not part of the gold rate.

How do I know my jewellery's purity?

Since 2021 gold jewellery sold in India must carry a BIS hallmark with a 6-character HUID code. You can check a HUID in the official BIS Care app, and record it with each item in Goldook.
