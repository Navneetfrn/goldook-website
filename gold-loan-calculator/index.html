Gold loan questions

How much gold loan can I get per gram?

Lenders lend a percentage of your gold's value, called loan-to-value (LTV). Many lend up to 75% of the value of the gold in your jewellery; RBI sets the maximum, and the limit can be higher for small loans. Stones and making charges are not counted. Ask your lender for their current LTV.

How is the gold loan EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months. Many gold loans also allow paying only interest each month and the principal at the end (bullet repayment).

Which is better, a bank or an NBFC gold loan?

Banks usually charge lower interest; NBFCs are often faster with less paperwork. Compare the interest rate, processing fee, LTV and what happens if gold prices fall before you choose.
