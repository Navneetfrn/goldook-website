For professionals

Serve Goldook customers with Goldook Pro

Goldook Pro is the work app for the professionals our customers rely on. Apply in a few minutes; our team checks every professional before they can take work.

Lawyers

Customers ask for a legally executed gift deed when they give gold to family, or for help finalising and registering their Will. We assign each request to a verified advocate in that state.

  1. ApplyWith your Bar Council enrolment number and the states you serve. We verify it.
  2. Get jobs by emailDownload the draft deed or Will with all the details filled in.
  3. Execute and uploadPrepare the deed on stamp paper, or finalise the Will and arrange signing before two witnesses (and registration if asked). Upload the signed copy and enter your fee.
  4. Get paidThe customer pays in the app; we pay you your fee less our platform fee.

Jewellers

Customers book gold polishing and repair with jewellers near them. Only shops we have checked are listed.

  1. ApplyYour shop, services and starting prices, with BIS registration or GSTIN if you have them.
  2. Get listedOnce approved, customers in your city see your shop in the app.
  3. Receive bookingsThe customer pays us a booking fee; you get an email with their details and the item.
  4. Do the workAgree the work and your price with the customer and get paid by them directly.
