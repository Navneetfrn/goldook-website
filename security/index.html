Security

How we keep you and your data safe

Your gold records are private. Here is what protects them, and what you can do too.

Encrypted everywhere

Every connection to Goldook uses HTTPS (TLS), so nobody on the network can read what you send. Your data is stored encrypted on our servers.

Only you see your items

Database rules let each person read only their own items, photos and bills. Our own team sees just a holding range, never your list.

Locked on your phone

Lock the app with a PIN or your fingerprint, hide amounts with one tap, and confirm with your PIN before giving anything away.

Two-step admin access

Goldook's admin tools need a code from an authenticator app as well as a password-free sign-in, and every action is checked on the server.

Locked-down pages

Our pages only run Goldook's own code (Content Security Policy), can't be framed by other sites, and send nothing to third-party trackers.

Checked professionals

Every lawyer and jeweller is checked by our team before they can take work, and they see only the details needed for that job.

Stay safe

Goldook will never ask you for

  • Your app PIN or the sign-in code (OTP) we send you
  • Your password, card number, CVV or UPI PIN
  • Payment outside the app, or to a personal bank account
  • To install a screen-sharing or remote-control app

If anyone claiming to be from Goldook asks for these, it isn't us. Don't share anything, and tell us.

Report a problem

Found a security issue?

If you think you've found a weakness in Goldook, please tell us privately at support@goldook.com with the steps to reproduce it. We'll reply within two working days and fix confirmed issues as fast as we can. Please don't access other people's data or disrupt the service while testing.
