About us

Gold is how Indian families save. We help them look after it.

Most families keep their gold in a locker and its details in someone's memory. Bills get lost, weights are forgotten, and when it matters most (a loan, a wedding, an inheritance) nobody is quite sure what there is. Goldook exists to fix that.

Our mission

Help every family know, protect and pass on its gold.

We want any family, in any language, to be able to answer three questions in a minute: what gold do we own, what is it worth today, and who should it go to? Everything we build starts from those questions.

Our vision

The trusted home for a family's gold.

One private place that brings together the record of what you own and the services gold needs over a lifetime: fair loans, legal paperwork, care and repair, and safe ways to buy and insure it, from people we have checked.

What we believe

Our values

Your privacy first

What you own is nobody else's business. Your list is yours alone; we design every feature so that we see as little as possible.

Honest numbers

We show where prices come from, say when a figure is an estimate, and show any fee in full before you pay it.

Your gold stays yours

We never take, hold, lend against or sell your gold. Services happen between you and people we have checked.

Made for all of India

English and 16 Indian languages, with numbers in rupees and grams, the way families actually talk about gold.

Checked professionals

Every lawyer, jeweller and agent on Goldook is checked by our team before they can start.

Free at the core

Keeping a record of your gold, its value and your nominees is free, and will stay that way.

How we make money

Plainly

Goldook earns only when you choose a paid service: a platform fee on legal gift deeds and Wills prepared by our advocates, and a small booking fee when you book a jeweller. We do not sell your data, and we do not lend money.
